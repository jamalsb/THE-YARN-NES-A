Get in Touch 💌

Have a question about a crochet tutorial, knitting pattern, yarn guide, or a product mentioned on The Yarn Nest? We welcome reader feedback, corrections, and suggestions for future craft guides.

For general questions, content feedback, affiliate-link concerns, or collaboration enquiries, email us at info@theyarnnest.shop. Please include the article title or page address if your message is about a specific guide.

What can you contact us about?

  • Questions or corrections about crochet and knitting tutorials.
  • Suggestions for beginner-friendly yarn projects and buying guides.
  • Feedback about product recommendations or affiliate links.
  • Website accessibility, broken pages, or other site issues.

How to write a helpful message

If you are reporting a problem with an article, tell us the page title and the section you are referring to. For a product-link question, include the product name and the page where you found the recommendation. This helps us understand the issue without asking you to repeat the details.

The Yarn Nest publishes independent crochet and knitting information for readers with different experience levels. We value clear corrections and practical suggestions, especially when a tutorial step, yarn detail, or page link could be clearer. You can also read our Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure to learn how site information and affiliate links are handled.
