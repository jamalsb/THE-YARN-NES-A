Easy Chunky Knit Blanket Pattern

By The Yarn Nest • October 15, 2024

There is nothing better than curling up with a soft, chunky knit blanket. The good news is, you don't need to be an expert knitter to make one! This beginner-friendly pattern uses thick yarn and large needles, meaning it works up incredibly fast.

In this guide, I will walk you through the exact materials you need and the simple steps to create your own cozy blanket in just a weekend.

Materials You Will Need

  • Yarn: 6-8 skeins of super chunky (weight 6) yarn. (I recommend a soft chenille or merino blend).
  • Needles: Size US 50 (25mm) circular knitting needles.
  • Tools: A large tapestry needle for weaving in ends, and scissors.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Cast On: Cast on 50 stitches using the long-tail cast-on method. This will give you a blanket that is roughly 40x50 inches.

2. Knit Every Row: This is the easiest part! Knit every single stitch on every row. This is called the "Garter Stitch" and it creates a beautiful, textured look that doesn't curl at the edges.

3. Keep Going: Continue knitting until your blanket reaches your desired length, or until you run out of yarn.

4. Bind Off: Bind off all stitches loosely so the edge remains stretchy.

5. Weave in Ends: Use your tapestry needle to weave in any loose ends.

Final Thoughts

This is one of the most rewarding projects for a beginner. It’s perfect for a movie marathon weekend. Happy knitting!
