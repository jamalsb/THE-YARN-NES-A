Best Yarn for Beginners: Top 3 Choices

By The Yarn Nest • October 24, 2024

Starting your crochet journey is exciting, but walking into a yarn store can be overwhelming. There are hundreds of textures, weights, and colors. Choosing the wrong yarn can make your first project frustrating.

In this guide, I’ll share the top 3 yarns that are perfect for beginners based on my personal experience.

1. The Best Overall: Lion Brand Wool-Ease

This is my absolute favorite for beginners. It is a blend of acrylic and wool, which means it is durable but still soft. It doesn't split easily, making it perfect for learning basic stitches.

2. The Best Budget Option: Red Heart Super Saver

If you want to practice without spending much money, this is the way to go. It is a bit scratchy at first, but it softens after washing. It comes in hundreds of colors.

3. The Best Premium Choice: Paintbox Simply DK

If you are willing to spend a little more for high-quality, soft yarn, Paintbox is amazing. The color palette is beautiful and it glides on the hook perfectly.

Final Thoughts

Don't be afraid to make mistakes. Buy a few skeins of Wool-Ease and start practicing your slip knots and single crochets. Happy crafting!
