Best Yarn for Beginners: Top 3 Choices

By The Yarn Nest • October 24, 2024

Starting your crochet or knitting journey is an exciting time! However, walking into a yarn store can feel overwhelming. There are hundreds of textures, weights, and colors. If you choose the wrong yarn, it can make your first project frustrating.

You want a yarn that is easy to work with, doesn't split easily, and is affordable. In this guide, I will share my top 3 yarn choices for beginners, based on my personal experience.

1. The Best Overall: Lion Brand Wool-Ease

This is my absolute favorite for beginners. It is a blend of acrylic and wool, which means it is durable, warm, but still very soft. It doesn't split easily, making it perfect for learning basic stitches like single crochet or the garter stitch.

  • Pros: Affordable, easy to find, machine washable.
  • Cons: Contains some wool, so check for allergies.

2. The Best Budget Option: Red Heart Super Saver

If you want to practice without spending much money, this is the way to go. It is a 100% acrylic yarn that comes in hundreds of colors. It feels a bit scratchy at first, but it softens significantly after washing.

  • Pros: Extremely cheap, huge variety of colors, very durable.
  • Cons: Can feel rough on the hands at first.

3. The Best Premium Choice: Paintbox Simply DK

If you are willing to spend a little more for high-quality, soft yarn, Paintbox is amazing. The color palette is incredibly beautiful, and it glides on the hook perfectly. It is a joy to work with.

  • Pros: Very soft, beautiful colors, great stitch definition.
  • Cons: Slightly more expensive than acrylics.

Final Thoughts

Don't be afraid to make mistakes. Buy a few skeins of the budget-friendly Red Heart Super Saver to practice your slip knots and single crochets, and once you feel confident, move on to the softer Wool-Ease. Happy crafting!
