## Google Search quality and technical checklist
- Prioritize helpful, original, people-first content with clear authorship and trustworthy sourcing.
- Avoid publishing many thin, repetitive AI-generated pages.
- Ensure mobile usability, fast loading, crawlable HTML text, descriptive titles, useful meta descriptions, canonical URLs, working internal links, XML sitemap, and correct robots settings.
- Use appropriate Article and Breadcrumb structured data where valid; validate it.
- Monitor Google Search Console indexing, queries, clicks, impressions, and page experience.
- Avoid keyword cannibalization: one main search intent per article; merge or differentiate overlapping pages.
- Do not promise first place or a guaranteed ranking.

## Pinterest and image-generation playbook
For every suitable article:
1. Inspect current Pinterest search suggestions and recent pins for visual trends, colour palettes, formats, and project types.
2. Treat pins as inspiration, not assets to reuse. Do not reproduce a specific creator's pattern or image.
3. Create 3–5 original vertical pin concepts per article, typically 2:3 composition, with a clear focal project and space for short editable title text.
4. Generate images that accurately depict the project and materials. Avoid malformed crochet stitches, impossible hooks, extra fingers, fake brand labels, or unreadable AI text.
5. Prefer no embedded text in generated images; add the final title in a design editor.
6. Use distinct compositions for each pin, descriptive filenames, relevant alt text, and destination links to the matching article.
7. Track saves, outbound clicks, and which pin designs send readers to the site.

## Article quality checklist
- Answer the searcher's main question in the first section without filler.
- State the intended reader and skill level.
- Include materials, yarn category/weight, yardage, hook size, gauge where relevant.
- Use original, accurate project photos and close-ups; do not copy Pinterest pins or competitor images.
- Use numbered instructions, stitch abbreviations and explanations; include left-handed help only if actually supplied.
- Add troubleshooting, variations, care notes, and FAQs only when useful.
- Cite reliable sources for technical yarn standards and distinguish tested experience from sourced facts.
- Include internal links that genuinely help the reader.
- Check US SERP, search intent, competition, and existing-site overlap before drafting.
