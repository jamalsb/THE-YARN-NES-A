Top 5 Crochet Hooks for Arthritic Hands

By The Yarn Nest • October 26, 2024

Crocheting is a wonderful hobby, but if you suffer from arthritis or hand fatigue, holding a thin metal hook for hours can become painful. The good news is that ergonomic crochet hooks can make a massive difference.

These hooks are designed with thicker, softer handles that reduce the pressure on your joints. In this guide, I’ll share the top 5 best crochet hooks for arthritic hands.

1. Clover Amour Crochet Hooks

The Clover Amour series is considered the gold standard for ergonomic hooks. The handles are made of soft rubber, which is incredibly comfortable to hold and prevents your hands from cramping.

2. Boye Ergonomic Crochet Hooks

These hooks have a specially designed comfort grip that is easy to hold. The colored handles also make it easy to identify the hook size at a glance.

3. Addi Swing Crochet Hooks

If you prefer a more colorful and highly ergonomic design, the Addi Swing is great. The handle is specifically shaped to support your hand in a natural position.

4. Furls Streamline Wood Hooks

If you love the feel of natural wood, Furls makes beautiful, lightweight hooks. They are smooth and warm to the touch, which is great for sensitive hands.

5. Tulip Etimo Rose

These are premium hooks with an ultra-smooth finish. The slightly thicker handle helps reduce tension in your grip while the hook itself glides effortlessly through the yarn.

Final Thoughts

Investing in a good ergonomic hook is the best thing you can do for your hands. Start with the Clover Amour as it is universally loved by crocheters with arthritis. Happy crocheting!
