How to Hold a Crochet Hook Properly

By The Yarn Nest • October 20, 2024

If you are new to crochet, one of the first challenges you will face is figuring out how to hold the hook. Holding it the wrong way can cause hand cramps and make your stitches uneven.

There is no single "right" way to hold a crochet hook, but there are two main methods that work best for most people. In this guide, we’ll cover both so you can find what feels most comfortable for you.

1. The Pencil Grip (Best for Control)

Just like holding a pencil, you hold the crochet hook between your thumb and index finger, resting it against your middle finger. This grip gives you excellent control over the yarn and is great for detailed work.

Who is it for? People who already know how to hold a pen comfortably. It might feel awkward at first, but it reduces wrist strain.

2. The Knife Grip (Best for Speed)

This is the most common method. You hold the hook like you would hold a knife to cut vegetables. Your thumb rests on the flat part of the hook, and your index finger guides the tip. Your hand moves from the wrist rather than the fingers.

Who is it for? People who want to crochet faster. It allows for larger movements and can be less tiring for long projects.

Which Grip Should You Choose?

The best way to find out is to try both! Grab a ball of yarn and practice a few basic stitches with each method. Whichever one causes less pain in your hands and feels more natural is the right one for you.

Final Thoughts

Don't worry if it feels clumsy at first. Muscle memory takes time to build. Practice for 15 minutes a day, and soon you will be crocheting without even thinking about how you are holding the hook. Happy crafting!
